2025 Canadian annual employment-income and disposable-cash estimate

Fixed to finalized full-year 2025 rules · Basic employee scenarios · All provinces and territories

Scope and important limitations

This is a simplified 2025 annual estimate for a single employee age 19 to 64, not a tax return, payroll-withholding calculation, or tax, legal, payroll, or financial advice. Actual tax depends on deductions, credits, benefits, residency, payroll frequency, personal circumstances, and CRA or Revenu Québec assessment. Confirm RRSP room on your latest Notice of Assessment or CRA account. Dividends, capital gains, and self-employment are not modeled.
