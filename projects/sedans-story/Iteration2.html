02 / The market shift

A market that looks different.

First define the vehicle categories, then show the national trend, then interpret it. The final evidence visuals will be built from verified data.

Reveal 01 / Define

What are we comparing?

"Sedan" is Pierre's preferred type. Statistics Canada's broader categories are passenger cars and multi-purpose vehicles.

Reveal 02 / Measure

How has the market changed?

Use annual registration evidence to make the shift visible. Distinguish vehicles already registered from new registrations.

Reveal 03 / Interpret

Does a smaller share mean gone?

No. A changing mix does not mean passenger cars literally disappear. It leads to the question of demand and supply.

Same decision. Bigger question.

Pierre still chooses according to his own budget and needs. Now he also asks how the selection available to him took shape.

05 / The takeaway

Changed.
Not gone.

Passenger cars have changed position in Canada's market, while SUVs and crossovers have grown in prominence. Explaining why requires evidence on both buyers and automakers.

Pierre's question opens the investigation. It does not decide the answer.
